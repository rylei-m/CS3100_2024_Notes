## Critical Section
- Counter++
  - Load reg1, counter
  - inc reg1
  - loud counter reg1
- Counter--
  - load reg2, counter
  - decrement reg2
  - load counter reg2
- Race Condition: Outcome depends on the specific ordering of instructions
- Entry Section
  - Request to enter the actual critical section
- Critical Section
  - Shared Resource is modified
- Exit Section
  - Notify the completion of the critical section
- Remainder Section
  - code noy related to critical section
  - everything after-wards that the process needs to do
- Requirements for Solution
  - Mutual Exclusion
    - Have to have the ability to limit access to shared resource/critical section
  - Progress
    - Selection of process to enter critical section cannot be postponed indefinitely
  - Bounded Waiting
    - Process can't wait indefinitely
